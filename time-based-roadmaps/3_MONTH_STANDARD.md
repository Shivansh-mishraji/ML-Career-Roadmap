# 📚 3-Month Standard: The Solid Foundation

**Target Audience:** You have 90 days. This is the ideal amount of time for a thorough, well-rounded preparation strategy before internship hiring season peaks.
**Goal:** Deep foundational math knowledge, advanced ML techniques, 3 solid projects, and rigorous DSA preparation.

> [!TIP]
> **Depth Expectation:** You should understand the underlying math. You need to know exactly how Backpropagation works, how to derive Linear Regression using Ordinary Least Squares, and how Attention mechanisms function in Transformers.

---

## 🛠️ What to Prepare (Topics)
1. **Math Foundations:** Linear Algebra (Matrices, Eigen-stuff), Calculus (Derivatives/Gradients), Probability (Distributions, Bayes).
2. **Advanced ML:** All classical ML + Time Series Forecasting, Anomaly Detection.
3. **Deep Learning:** PyTorch mastery, CNNs for vision, RNNs/Transformers for text.
4. **DSA:** Data Structures and Algorithms (Crucial for 3-month timelines as top tech companies *will* test you on this).

## 📚 From Where to Prepare (Resources)
- **Math:** [3Blue1Brown (Essence of Linear Algebra & Calculus)](https://www.youtube.com/c/3blue1brown) and [Khan Academy Statistics](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/statistics-probability).
- **Deep Learning Theory:** [DeepLearning.AI Specialization by Andrew Ng](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/deep-learning).
- **DSA:** [NeetCode 150](https://neetcode.io/practice) (Focus on Arrays, Hash Maps, Two Pointers, Trees).

---

## 🗓️ 12-Week Breakdown

### Month 1: The Foundations (Math, Python, SQL)
- **Weeks 1-2 (Math & Python):** Solidify Linear Algebra. Master NumPy vectorization (no `for` loops!). Master Pandas.
- **Weeks 3-4 (SQL & EDA):** Learn Window functions and complex CTEs in SQL. Build a massive Exploratory Data Analysis project on a complex dataset (e.g., Housing Prices with 80+ features).
- **DSA Habit:** 1 LeetCode problem every single day.

### Month 2: Machine Learning Mastery
- **Weeks 5-6 (Supervised):** Linear/Logistic Regression, SVMs, Decision Trees, Random Forests, XGBoost.
- **Weeks 7-8 (Unsupervised & Time Series):** K-Means, DBSCAN, PCA. Learn ARIMA or Prophet for Time Series.
- **Action:** Build **Project 1** (Tabular Data) and focus heavily on Feature Engineering. Explain your feature choices mathematically.

### Month 3: Deep Learning, NLP, and Polish
- **Weeks 9-10 (Deep Learning):** Build a Neural Network from scratch using NumPy to understand backprop, then switch to PyTorch.
- **Week 11 (NLP/GenAI):** Understand Word2Vec, TF-IDF, and implement a RAG pipeline (see our [RAG Template](../interactive-projects/RAG_LLM_TEMPLATE.md)).
- **Week 12 (Deployment & Resumes):** Deploy your best model using FastAPI and Docker. Optimize your resume and LinkedIn.
