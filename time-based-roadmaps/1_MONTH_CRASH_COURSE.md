# 🚀 1-Month Crash Course: Survival Mode

**Target Audience:** You have an interview or an internship starting in exactly 30 days. You need to be functional *fast*.
**Goal:** Survive technical rounds, explain core ML concepts, and build 2 working projects.

> [!WARNING]
> **What to Skip (Depth limitation):** Do NOT try to learn the math behind Backpropagation, Eigenvectors, or Advanced Calculus right now. You do not have time. Focus strictly on *how to use* the tools (APIs) rather than *how they are built from scratch*.

---

## 🛠️ What to Prepare (Topics)
1. **Python for Data:** Pandas and Scikit-Learn only. Skip deep OOP concepts.
2. **Core ML Algorithms:** Linear/Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost.
3. **GenAI (High ROI):** Use LangChain/OpenAI APIs (no fine-tuning).
4. **SQL:** Basic aggregations and JOINs.

## 📚 From Where to Prepare (Resources)
- **Python/Pandas:** [Kaggle Python Micro-Course](https://www.kaggle.com/learn/python) (takes 4 hours).
- **Machine Learning Intuition:** [StatQuest with Josh Starmer](https://www.youtube.com/user/joshstarmer) on YouTube. Watch the videos on Random Forests and XGBoost.
- **SQL:** [LeetCode Top 50 SQL](https://leetcode.com/studyplan/top-sql-50/) (Do the first 20).
- **Projects:** Use our [RAG_LLM_TEMPLATE](../interactive-projects/RAG_LLM_TEMPLATE.md) for a quick, impressive win.

---

## 🗓️ Weekly Breakdown

### Week 1: Data Wrangling & SQL (The Bread & Butter)
- **How:** Spend 3 hours/day on Kaggle. 
- **Depth:** You must be able to load a CSV, handle missing values (`df.fillna()`), group data (`df.groupby()`), and merge tables (`pd.merge()`).
- **Action:** Complete the Kaggle Pandas course. Do 5 SQL queries on LeetCode every day.

### Week 2: Scikit-Learn & Core ML
- **How:** Don't write algorithms from scratch. Use `sklearn`.
- **Depth:** Understand the difference between fit() and predict(). Know what Train/Test split is. Understand metrics: Accuracy vs. F1-Score.
- **Action:** Take the Titanic dataset, clean it, and train a Random Forest model.

### Week 3: High-Value Project Building
- **How:** Pick an API-driven project. Recruiters love GenAI.
- **Depth:** You don't need to understand Transformer attention heads. You just need to know how to send a prompt to an LLM and parse the response.
- **Action:** Build a Streamlit app using our [RAG Template](../interactive-projects/RAG_LLM_TEMPLATE.md). It takes 1-2 days if you follow the template. Push it to GitHub.

### Week 4: Interview Prep & Polish
- **How:** Practice speaking out loud.
- **Depth:** Be able to explain the Bias-Variance tradeoff, Overfitting, and why you chose Random Forest over Logistic Regression in your project.
- **Action:** Read the [Mentor Networking Tips](../internship-prep/MENTOR_TIPS_AND_NETWORKING.md) and optimize your resume. Send 10 cold emails.
